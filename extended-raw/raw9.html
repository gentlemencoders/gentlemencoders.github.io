Apple

iPhone ProRAW is not supported by V9 because ProRAW is not a true RAW file (it's linearized)
Regular iPhone DNGs (true RAW files) are supported by V9
Important Note: RAW 9 works with regular RAW DNG, but not linearized DNG.
So, if your camera can shoot DNG files natively, they should work with RAW 9.
